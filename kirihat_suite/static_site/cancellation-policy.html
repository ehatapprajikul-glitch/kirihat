3. Seller & Vendor Cancellation

3.1. Before Accepting Order

Sellers and Vendors can cancel (reject) orders before accepting them if:

  • Product is out of stock
  • Unable to fulfill the order
  • Product information is incorrect
  • Technical issues prevent order processing

3.2. After Accepting Order

⚠️ Important

Once a Seller or Vendor accepts an order, they CANNOT cancel it.

Sellers and Vendors must fulfill accepted orders. Repeated cancellations after acceptance may result in:

  • Account suspension
  • Penalties or fines
  • Removal from the platform
  • Loss of selling privileges

3.3. Emergency Situations

In exceptional circumstances (natural disasters, emergencies, force majeure), Sellers/Vendors may request order cancellation by contacting Kirihat support immediately.

4. Rider Cancellation

4.1. When Riders Can Cancel

Delivery riders may cancel orders in the following situations:

📵

Customer Unreachable

Customer phone is switched off, not answering calls, or unreachable after multiple attempts

❌

Customer Unavailable

Customer is not present at the delivery address when rider arrives

🚫

Customer Refuses Delivery

Customer refuses to accept the order without valid reason

🗺️

Wrong/Inaccessible Address

Incorrect address provided or location is inaccessible

💸

Payment Issues (COD)

Customer doesn't have cash ready for COD orders

⚠️

Fraudulent/Fake Order

Rider suspects the order is fake or fraudulent

⚠️ Consequences for Customers

If a rider cancels your order due to your unavailability or fraudulent activity:

  • The order will be cancelled immediately
  • You may not receive a full refund (cancellation fee may apply)
  • Your account may be flagged or suspended
  • Repeated incidents may result in permanent account ban

4.2. Rider Responsibilities

Before cancelling an order, riders must:

  • Make reasonable attempts to contact the customer
  • Wait at the delivery location for a reasonable time
  • Try to resolve any delivery issues
  • Document the reason for cancellation
  • Report the cancellation to Kirihat immediately

If a seller or vendor cancels your order:

9. Cancellation vs Return

Understanding the Difference

❌ Cancellation

Timing: BEFORE product is shipped

When to use: Change of mind, ordered by mistake, found better price

Process: Click "Cancel Order" in app

Refund: Immediate (24-48 hours for UPI)

Fees: Usually free, may apply in late cancellations

This Policy

↩️ Return

Timing: AFTER product is delivered

When to use: Defective product, wrong item, not as described

Process: Request return, rider pickup arranged

Refund: 2 days after pickup + quality check

Fees: May apply based on reason
